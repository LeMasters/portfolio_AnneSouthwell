A web app where students join a queue for help during open lab hours, and TAs mark tickets done and leave notes. I pitched it from my IMS213 TA hours, when the sticky-note queue kept falling off the whiteboard, and wrote the TA-side views. It handled about 130 tickets in a four-week pilot.
