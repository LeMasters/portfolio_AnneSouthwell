For a Game Design Club workshop I brought a roll of adding-machine paper, a marker and a bag of red paper buoys. One person tapped a rhythm. A partner placed buoys on the strip and slid the whole thing under a pencil held still. Then we made the same edit with my Godot beat-placement tool from Undertow. A participant moved a buoy one square and asked whether she'd moved the note or changed the tempo. I added a second pass to the handout: keep the buoys still and pull the paper faster. The two gestures gave us names for two different controls.
